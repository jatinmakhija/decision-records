## Context

Three vendor contracts last quarter were signed by a single director and missed finance review.

## Rationale

A second approver catches scope and renewal terms without adding a committee for small purchases.

## Alternatives

- Raise the single-approver limit to 25000: faster, and it keeps the miss we just had.
- Require the CFO for every purchase: safe, and it blocks the team on laptop orders.

## Consequences

Purchases at or under 10000 stay with one approver. Above that, work waits for a second yes. Directors need a written fallback for urgent security buys.

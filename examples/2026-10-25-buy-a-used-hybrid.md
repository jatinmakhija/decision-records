## Context

The current car needs a repair that costs more than it is worth. The commute is 12 km each way.

## Rationale

A used hybrid cuts the purchase price by about 40 percent and matches the short commute. We can revisit when the kids start school.

## Alternatives

- New hybrid: quieter, and it comes with a warranty we do not need for this mileage.
- Keep repairing the current car: lowest cash outlay this month, and the repair risk stays.

## Consequences

We spend from savings this month. Fuel cost drops. A larger repair on the used car is a risk we accept.

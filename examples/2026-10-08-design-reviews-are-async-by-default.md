## Context

Standing review meetings pulled in people across time zones. Most comments were typed after the call anyway.

## Rationale

A written review leaves a record and lets people respond when they can. A live meeting is still available when the written thread stalls.

## Alternatives

- Keep the weekly live review: familiar, and it costs an hour for people who have nothing to add.
- Review only inside the ticket: close to the work, and easy to miss.

## Consequences

The author posts a short note and waits two working days. A live review is scheduled only when someone asks for one. We accept slower turnaround on urgent work unless someone calls the meeting.

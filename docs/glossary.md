# Glossary

These words appear as fields and as headings. The one-line hint is what the editor shows.

**Decision.** The choice, in one sentence, so a stranger knows what was chosen.

**Context.** The situation and constraints at the time. What was true, not why you chose.

**Rationale.** Why this option won.

**Alternatives.** Options you seriously considered, and why you did not pick them.

**Consequences.** What follows, including costs, follow-up work, and downsides you accept.

**Deciders.** People accountable for the choice.

**Participants.** People consulted or present who did not decide.

**Recorded by.** Who wrote the record down. This is not a claim that they decided or attended.

**Sources.** Where the facts came from: links, tickets, meeting notes.

**Supersedes.** Earlier decisions this one replaces.

**Superseded by.** The newer decision that replaced this one.

**Status.** Where the choice sits. `proposed` is not final. `decided` is the choice, including a choice not to do something. `rejected` means a proposal did not pass. `superseded` means a later record replaced it. `revoked` means it was withdrawn with no replacement.

**Tags.** Optional labels such as `engineering` or `personal`. A record can carry more than one. Tags are not a fixed list of categories.

**Schema version.** The version of this file format. It is not the version of the `decision` program.
